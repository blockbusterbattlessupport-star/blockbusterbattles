Blockbuster Battles is a free-to-play arena action game for Android and iPhone. Pick a hero or villain, fight waves of enemies across Superhero, Horror, Sci-Fi, Adventure and Western worlds, choose new powers every level, and take down the boss with a Final Shot.

Collect 14 characters, upgrade them with cards, research their powers, raise pets and build up your HQ.
