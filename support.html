Common questions

I bought something but didn't get it. Close and reopen the Game while online; unfinished purchases are delivered on the next launch. For one-time items (No Ads, VIP, Solara Boost, Mega Bundle) use Settings > Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play (play.google.com > Order history) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

The video ad didn't give me my reward. Rewards are given only when the video plays to the end. If no video is available, try again in a minute.

How do I turn off personalised ads? Settings > Personalised Ads. Buying No Ads or VIP removes ads between battles.

I changed phones. Can I keep my progress? Progress is saved on the device, so it does not move to a new phone. Purchased one-time items can be restored with Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data? Uninstall the Game to delete everything stored on your device. To reset the advertising ID used by ad partners, use your phone's ad settings (see the privacy policy).
