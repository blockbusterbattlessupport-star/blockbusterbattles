Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Blockbuster Battles (the "Game"), made by FilmBattles ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. We do not run accounts or servers for the Game: your progress is stored on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, the player name you choose and a random player ID are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource) and its ad network partners, including Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, these partners collect from your device:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iPhone if you allow tracking)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size) and your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) is derived
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, watched, clicked) and diagnostic data about ad performance

This information is used to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, to show ads that match your interests (personalised ads).

Your choices: the Game asks whether you allow personalised ads, and you can change this at any time in Settings > Personalised Ads. If you decline, you still see ads but they are not personalised, and we tell our partners not to sell or share your data. On iPhone, the Game also asks for Apple's tracking permission; you can change it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads. Buying No Ads or VIP removes ads between battles.

Partner privacy policies: Unity, ironSource.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The store tells the Game which items you own so they can be delivered and restored.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are requested with the "not child-directed" setting. If you believe a child has used the Game in a way that shared personal information, contact us and we will help.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. The advertising partners above receive the data described in section 2 to provide their services. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Security and retention

Data sent by the Game's partners travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Partners keep data according to their own policies. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Because we do not hold your data ourselves, the quickest way is to use the choices in section 2; for anything else, contact us and we will help you reach the relevant partner.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
